# Student Handbook 2026/27

## 1. Welcome

This handbook explains the academic rules, key dates and support services for all undergraduate students in the Faculty of Computing. Read it carefully at the start of the year and keep it for reference. If any part of this handbook conflicts with the University Regulations, the regulations take precedence.

## 2. Academic Calendar

Orientation and induction week runs from [[-14d]] to [[-10d]]. Semester 1 teaching begins on [[-7d|long]]. Course registration and module selection close on [[+4d]]; changes after this date require approval from the Head of Department. Reading week is scheduled for [[+40d]] to [[+44d]], and there is no scheduled teaching during that week. Final examinations for Semester 1 will be held from [[+68d]] to [[+79d]]. Examination results will be released on [[+110d]]. Semester 2 teaching begins on [[+124d|long]].

## 3. Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 80% in every module to be eligible to sit the final examination. Attendance is recorded at every lecture, tutorial and laboratory session. Absences due to illness must be supported by a medical certificate submitted to the Faculty Office within seven days of returning to university. Students whose attendance falls below 80% receive a written warning and may be barred from the examination.

## 4. Assessment and Grading

Each module is assessed through continuous assessment and a final examination. Unless the module handbook states otherwise, continuous assessment contributes 40% and the final examination contributes 60% of the module mark. The pass mark for each module is 40%, and students must also achieve at least 35% in the final examination. Marks are converted to grade points on a 4.0 scale, and the grade point average (GPA) is calculated by weighting each module by its credit value.

## 5. Coursework Submission

All coursework must be submitted through the virtual learning environment (VLE) before the published deadline, which is 23:59 on the due date unless stated otherwise. Email submissions are not accepted. Late submissions incur a penalty of 5% of the total available marks for each working day, up to a maximum of five working days. Submissions received more than five working days late will receive a mark of zero. Technical problems with the VLE must be reported to the IT Service Desk before the deadline.

## 6. Mitigating Circumstances

If illness, bereavement or other serious circumstances beyond your control affect your ability to submit on time or attend an examination, you may apply for mitigating circumstances. Applications must be made using the mitigating circumstances form within seven days of the assessment deadline and must be supported by independent evidence such as a medical certificate. Approved applications may receive an extension of up to 14 days or a deferred examination without penalty. Workload from other modules, holidays and computer problems are not accepted as mitigating circumstances.

## 7. Academic Misconduct

Plagiarism, collusion, contract cheating and fabrication of data are serious academic offences. All written work is checked with similarity software, and submissions with a similarity index above 20% are reviewed by the module leader. Confirmed cases are referred to the Academic Misconduct Panel, which may award a mark of zero for the assessment or the whole module. Repeated offences can lead to suspension from the university.

## 8. Support Services

The Student Support Centre offers academic advising, counselling and disability support. Every student is assigned a personal tutor who should be contacted for advice about academic progress. The library provides workshops on referencing, research skills and academic writing throughout the semester, and the first referencing workshop takes place on [[+9d]].
