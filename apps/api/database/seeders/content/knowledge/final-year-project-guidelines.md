# Final Year Project Guidelines

## 1. Overview

The final year project is an individual piece of work worth 40 credits. It gives you the opportunity to investigate a computing problem in depth, apply the knowledge gained during your degree and demonstrate independent professional skills. The project is assessed on the quality of the product, the dissertation and the viva voce examination.

## 2. Supervision

Each student is assigned a supervisor who approves the project proposal and provides guidance throughout the year. Students should meet their supervisor at least once every two weeks and keep a record of each meeting in the project log. The supervisor advises on direction and scope but does not write or correct the dissertation.

## 3. Project Milestones

The project proposal must be submitted to your supervisor by [[+11d|long]]. The ethics approval form must be completed before any data is collected from human participants, and no later than [[+25d]]. The interim progress report is due on [[+60d]]. Project demonstrations take place in the week of [[+150d]], and the final dissertation deadline is [[+164d]] at 12:00 noon. The viva voce examination will be held on [[+178d]].

## 4. Project Proposal

The proposal is a document of no more than 1,500 words that states the problem, aims and objectives, scope, methodology and expected outcomes of the project. It must include a project plan, a preliminary literature review and an initial risk assessment. Proposals that are too broad or lack a clear research question will be returned for revision.

## 5. Interim Progress Report

The interim report describes the work completed so far, the results of the literature review and any changes to the plan. It should be between 3,000 and 4,000 words. The supervisor provides written feedback within two weeks, and the report contributes 10% of the final project mark.

## 6. Dissertation Format

The final dissertation must be between 10,000 and 12,000 words, excluding references and appendices. It must contain an abstract, introduction, literature review, methodology, design and implementation, testing and evaluation, conclusion and references. Use the IEEE referencing style consistently. The dissertation must be submitted as a single PDF through the VLE, together with the source code in a ZIP file.

## 7. Viva Voce Examination

The viva is a 30-minute oral examination with your supervisor and a second marker. You will give a short presentation and a live demonstration of the product, followed by questions about your design decisions, testing and evaluation. The viva confirms that the work is your own and contributes 15% of the final mark.

## 8. Assessment Criteria

The final mark is made up of the interim report (10%), the product and demonstration (35%), the dissertation (40%) and the viva (15%). Markers reward clear problem definition, critical use of literature, sound methodology, technical quality, thorough testing and honest evaluation of limitations.
